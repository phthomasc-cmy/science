Analogy Cheat Sheet
V
Voltage = Water Pressure (Volts)
The pump pushes water out at high pressure. Without pressure, water sits still.
A
Current = Flow Rate (Amps)
How many litres pass a spot every second—measured by a wheel inside the pipe (series).
Ω
Resistance = Narrow Pipe (Ohms)
Pinching the pipe slows the flow everywhere in the loop.
