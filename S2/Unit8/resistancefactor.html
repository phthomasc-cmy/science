3 Golden Rules Summary
1. Longer Wire → Higher Resistance: Directly proportional (R ∝ L). Double the length = double the resistance.
2. Thinner Wire → Higher Resistance: Inversely proportional (R ∝ 1 / A). Half the area = double the resistance.
3. Material Matters: Good conductors (like Copper) have low resistivity (ρ); alloys (like Nichrome) have high resistivity.
