Part A: True (T) or False (F) 是非題

1. Rainwater with a pH value lower than 5.6 is considered as acid rain.
中文翻譯：pH值低於5.6的雨水被認為是酸雨。
Answer 答案: True (T) 正確
🧪Science Knowledge 科學知識:
Normal rainwater naturally absorbs carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) from the air, making it slightly acidic with a pH of about 5.6. It is only called "acid rain" when the pH drops below 5.6 due to pollution.
正常的雨水會自然吸收空氣中的二氧化碳（$CO_2$），使其呈微酸性，pH值約為5.6。只有當污染導致pH值低於5.6時，才被稱為「酸雨」。

2. There is no acid rain in Hong Kong.
中文翻譯：香港沒有酸雨。
Answer 答案: False (F) 錯誤
🧪Science Knowledge 科學知識:
Hong Kong, as a highly developed city, experiences acid rain caused by vehicle emissions and regional industrial pollution.
香港作為高度發展的城市，會因為汽車廢氣和區域性工業污染而出現酸雨現象。

3. Carbon dioxide is an acidic gas that causes acid rain mainly.
中文翻譯：二氧化碳是主要導致酸雨的酸性氣體。
Answer 答案: False (F) 錯誤

Carbon dioxide is NOT the main cause. The main causes of acid rain are Sulphur Dioxide ($SO_2$) and Nitrogen Oxides ($NO_x$).
二氧化碳不是主要原因。導致酸雨的主要氣體是二氧化硫($SO_2$)和氮氧化物($NO_x$)。

Part B: Multiple Choices 選擇題

1. Which of the following shows the pH values of natural rainwater and distilled water?
中文翻譯：下列哪一項顯示了天然雨水和蒸餾水的pH值？
Answer 答案: C (Natural rainwater: 5.6, Distilled water: 7)
🧪Science Knowledge 科學知識:
Natural rainwater contains dissolved $CO_2$, making it slightly acidic (pH 5.6). Distilled water is pure $H_2O$ with no impurities, making it perfectly neutral (pH 7).
天然雨水含有溶解的二氧化碳，呈微酸性（pH 5.6）。蒸餾水是純淨的水（$H_2O$），沒有任何雜質，因此是完全中性的（pH 7）。

2. There are some brown solids formed on the surface of the air conditioner. Which of the following is the most likely cause?
中文翻譯：冷氣機表面形成了一些棕色固體。下列哪一項是最可能的原因？
Answer 答案: B (Acids in rainwater react with iron on the surface...)
雨水中的酸與冷氣機表面的鐵發生反應。
🧪Science Knowledge 科學知識:
The "brown solids" are rust (iron oxide). Acid rain speeds up the corrosion (rusting) of metals like iron. Air conditioners are encased in metal (iron/steel), not calcium carbonate (marble).

3. Which of the following gas(s) is / are found in clean rainwater? (1) oxygen, (2) nitrogen oxides, (3) carbon dioxide
中文翻譯：下列哪些氣體可以在潔淨的雨水中被發現？(1) 氧氣, (2) 氮氧化物, (3) 二氧化碳
Answer 答案: B ((1) and (3) only) 只有 (1) 和 (3)
🧪Science Knowledge 科學知識:
Clean air naturally contains Oxygen ($O_2$) and Carbon Dioxide ($CO_2$), so these dissolve into clean rain. Nitrogen Oxides ($NO_x$) are pollutants from cars/factories, so they are only found in polluted acid rain, not "clean" rain.
乾淨的空氣自然含有氧氣和少量二氧化碳，所以它們會溶解在乾淨的雨水中。氮氧化物是來自汽車/工廠的污染物，只存在於受污染的酸雨中，不存在於「潔淨」雨水中。

4. Which of the following can be used to remove acidic gases given out from power stations?
中文翻譯：下列哪一項可用來去除發電廠排放的酸性氣體？
Answer 答案: A (Limestone) 石灰石
🧪Science Knowledge 科學知識:
To remove an ACID, you need to use a BASE (alkali) to neutralize it. Limestone (calcium carbonate) is basic. Power stations use it in "scrubbers" to neutralize acidic sulphur dioxide gas.
要去除「酸」，你需要使用「鹼」來中和它。石灰石（碳酸鈣）是鹼性的。發電廠在「洗滌器」中使用它來中和酸性的二氧化硫氣體。
